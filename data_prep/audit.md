## Implications for the build

- `XXXX` is IU's own de-identification token; it is kept as-is in rendered text (it is not PHI) and the PHI detector must not flag it.
- Reports with neither Findings nor Impression are excluded from rendering.
- The large share of normal / exact-duplicate reports is why the split groups near-duplicates before stratifying (otherwise golden-set scores are inflated by memorised boilerplate).
